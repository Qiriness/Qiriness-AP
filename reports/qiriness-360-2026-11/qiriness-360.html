1 · DIAGNOSIS

Executive commercial diagnosis

Strengths

  • Once customers order twice they stay: 52% reach a 3rd order and 71% a 4th. The 26% of buyers with 2+ orders produce 54% of revenue.
  • Temps Sublime entrants rebuy at 30.4% within 180 days (baseline 18.8%); Exception and Éclat Parfait at about 25%.
  • Free channels acquire well: organic search, Google Shopping free listings (20% rebuy), email.
  • The €70 free-shipping threshold visibly lifts baskets (364 orders at €70–75 vs 188 at €65–70).

Weaknesses

  • 75% of new customers never place a second order.
  • Cohort quality is falling: orders per new customer within 180 days went from 1.47 to 1.21. The Nov–Jan holiday cohorts rebuy at only 13–18%.
  • About 95% of orders carry a markdown or code. The Temps Sublime Duo is listed at €179 and sells at €89.50.
  • Paid social cost about €59–98 per new customer, who were worth €45 at 180 days.

Top five recommendations

  • A 0–180-day second-purchase programme (post-purchase flows by entry range, with a 15% holdout). Base case: +€13k a year.
  • Always-on replenishment and routine completion at measured reorder windows. About 380 customers are due now; 428 Temps Sublime buyers lack the eye cream.
  • Lead Black Friday and acquisition with retention-quality entry products (TS Duo, Regard Sublime, Exception, Éclat Parfait), not wraps or LED devices.
  • Fewer, segmented campaigns (8–12 a month instead of about 31). Move effort into flows, which earn €6.8 per delivered email against €0.05 for campaigns.
  • Test promotions with holdouts: the welcome code shows no retention advantage. Prefer gift-with-purchase thresholds to extra percentage discounts.

6 · ACQUISITION & CRM

Where new customers come from, and what email does

First-touch channel from Shopify's customer journey (58–64% of orders have one). Meta spend was €9,690 for Nov 2025–Feb 2026: about €98 per tracked new customer, or about €59 scaled for untracked orders.

Klaviyo-attributed value incl. VAT. Klaviyo claims 24–61% of monthly DTC revenue; Shopify's last-touch data gives email 14% of orders. Treat this as attribution, not incrementality.

9 · NEXT 90 DAYS

First 90-day roadmap

Now, around Black Friday

  • Feature TS Duo, Regard Sublime, Exception and Éclat Parfait as entry products; keep LED for gifting.
  • Post-purchase flow v1 (TS / Source d'Eau / other) live before the BF cohort lands, with a 15% holdout.
  • Cap campaigns at 3 a week per profile; suppress profiles not engaged for 90 days.
  • Pause cold paid social until a test design exists.

Experiments, weeks 2–12

  • Replenishment reminder at p25: 50/50 split; success is ≥ +3 pts reorder within 60 days.
  • TS → eye cream completion: 80/20 holdout among the 428.
  • Welcome incentive: 15% code vs gift vs 10%, judged on 180-day value.
  • Cart add-on and win-back of at-risk repeaters, each with a holdout.
