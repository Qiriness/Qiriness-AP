chat.customers — One row per Shopify customer, as they are NOW (a snapshot, not a history: past states cannot be reconstructed). Marketplace orders (the handles listed in your instructions) create one synthetic customer per order, so exclude customers whose orders are all on those channels from any per-person metric.
  customer_id uuid
  created_at timestamp with time zone
  account_state text — ENABLED (has an account), INVITED, DISABLED. DISABLED is the default for guest checkouts, not a closed account.
  locale text
  country_code text
  number_of_orders integer — Lifetime order count as Shopify reports it.
  amount_spent numeric(12,2) — Lifetime spend as Shopify reports it (EUR).
  currency_code text
  last_order_at timestamp with time zone
  rfm_group text — Shopify RFM segment: CHAMPIONS, LOYAL, ACTIVE, NEW, PROMISING, NEEDS_ATTENTION, AT_RISK, ALMOST_LOST, DORMANT, PREVIOUSLY_LOYAL, PROSPECTS (never ordered).
  email_marketing_state text — SUBSCRIBED, NOT_SUBSCRIBED, UNSUBSCRIBED, INVALID. Current state only: unsubscribes over time are undercounted.
  on_email_marketing_list boolean

chat.fulfilment_timing — Per order: how long from placing the order to its first shipment. This is FULFILMENT time. Delivery time is not measurable: no carrier delivery data exists.
  order_id uuid
  processed_at timestamp with time zone
  first_fulfilled_at timestamp with time zone
  fulfilment_hours numeric — Hours from processed_at to first_fulfilled_at; null while the order has not shipped.
  carrier text — Carrier of the first shipment: one canonical upper-case name per carrier, however the fulfilment feed spelled it.
  fulfilment_count bigint
  destination_country_code text
  channel text
  return_status text
  total_refunded numeric(12,2)

chat.llm_usage — One row per AI model call by the support agent (spam, categorise, decompose, investigate, draft, embed). Tokens only: no prices are stored, so cost cannot be computed here. cached_input_tokens is part of input_tokens, never add them.
  ticket_id uuid
  pass text
  model text
  input_tokens integer
  cached_input_tokens integer
  output_tokens integer
  total_tokens integer
  call_count integer
  succeeded boolean
  error_kind text
  occurred_at timestamp with time zone

chat.order_lines — One row per line item of an order: what was sold. product_title is the title at the time of sale. Join products on shopify_product_id.
  order_id uuid
  line_index integer
  processed_at timestamp with time zone
  channel text
  cancelled_at timestamp with time zone
  product_title text
  variant_title text
  sku text
  shopify_product_id text
  quantity integer — Units ordered on this line.
  current_quantity integer — Units still on the order after removals and refunds.
  original_total numeric
  discounted_total numeric — Line revenue after line-level discounts (EUR), excluding shipping and order-level adjustments.

chat.orders — One row per Shopify order (all sales channels). Money is in currency_code (EUR). processed_at is when the order was placed (UTC). Join customers on customer_id, order_lines and fulfilment_timing on order_id, tickets on order_number (tickets.shopify_order_number is text).
  order_id uuid
  order_name text
  order_number integer
  customer_id uuid
  processed_at timestamp with time zone
  channel text — Stable sales channel handle: web = Online Store, shopify-draft-orders = Draft Orders, shop-72 = Shop app; the marketplace handles are listed in your instructions.
  channel_label text
  financial_status text — Shopify payment state: PAID, PARTIALLY_REFUNDED, REFUNDED, VOIDED, ...
  fulfillment_status text — Shopify fulfilment state: FULFILLED, UNFULFILLED, PARTIALLY_FULFILLED, ...
  return_status text
  order_status text — Derived lifecycle: fulfilled, delivered, cancelled, return_refund_completed, ... "delivered" is only set on the few orders where a delivery was recorded; do not use it to measure delivery.
  cancelled_at timestamp with time zone — Set when the order was cancelled. Exclude cancelled orders from sales figures.
  cancel_reason text
  closed_at timestamp with time zone
  currency_code text
  subtotal_price numeric(12,2)
  total_discounts numeric(12,2)
  total_shipping_price numeric(12,2)
  total_tax numeric(12,2)
  total_price numeric(12,2) — Order total including tax and shipping, after discounts, before refunds.
  total_refunded numeric — Amount refunded so far; 0 when nothing was refunded.
  destination_country_code text
  destination_country text
  line_count integer
  units bigint — Total quantity across the order's line items.
  tags text[] — Merchant tags. Marketplace orders may carry the marketplace's own tag.

chat.products — The Shopify catalogue as it is now. status: active, draft, archived, unlisted. For what sold, use order_lines.
  product_id uuid
  shopify_product_id text
  title text
  status text
  vendor text
  product_type text
  tags text[]
  available_stock integer — Units in stock across variants at the last sync; null when not tracked.
  variant_count integer
  published_at timestamp with time zone
  created_at timestamp with time zone

chat.promotions — Shopify discounts, one row per discount. method: code or automatic. discount_usage_count is how many times it has been used. The codes themselves are not exposed.
  promotion_id uuid
  title text
  method text
  discount_type text
  status text
  summary text
  starts_at timestamp with time zone
  ends_at timestamp with time zone
  usage_limit integer
  discount_usage_count integer
  applies_once_per_customer boolean
  code_count integer
  offerable_in_replies boolean

chat.shop — One row: the shop. iana_timezone is where a day starts for reporting. The VIP rule is net spend above vip_min_spend AND more than vip_min_orders orders inside the last vip_window_months (all null = no VIP rule set). The customers it admits are listed in chat.vip_customers.
  shop_name text
  iana_timezone text
  vip_min_spend numeric(12,2)
  vip_min_orders integer
  vip_window_months integer

chat.ticket_drafts — Replies the AI agent drafted. Nothing is sent automatically. status is the human review decision (pending, approved, edited, rejected); was_rewritten is true when a reviewer changed the text; auto_send_eligible is whether it WOULD have qualified for auto-send.
  draft_id uuid
  ticket_id uuid
  source_verdict text
  disposition text
  level smallint
  language text
  status text
  checks_passed boolean
  auto_send_eligible boolean
  was_rewritten boolean
  model text
  drafted_at timestamp with time zone

chat.ticket_investigations — The AI agent's investigation of a ticket, one per inbound message investigated. verdict: answerable (the agent could answer), needs_customer_input, needs_human.
  investigation_id uuid
  ticket_id uuid
  verdict text
  proposed_level smallint
  established_count integer
  missing_count integer
  model text
  investigated_at timestamp with time zone

chat.ticket_message_counts — Messages per ticket: total, and how many came from the customer (inbound).
  ticket_id uuid
  message_count bigint
  inbound_count bigint
  latest_inbound_at timestamp with time zone
  latest_outbound_at timestamp with time zone

chat.ticket_reply_times — First-response time per ticket: first customer message to the first reply after it. reply_hours is null when the desk has not replied from the synced mailbox.
  ticket_id uuid
  first_inbound_at timestamp with time zone
  first_outbound_at timestamp with time zone
  reply_hours numeric
  inbound_month date

chat.tickets — One row per customer-support email thread. Coverage starts when the mailbox sync began: use min(first_message_at) as the start of the data and never report a period before it. Join orders on shopify_order_number = orders.order_name or the order number as text.
  ticket_id uuid
  status text — open, awaiting_customer, awaiting_human, resolved, closed.
  category text — Subject: order, delivery, product, product_stock, return_exchange, promotions, payment, account, b2b, careers, partner_collaboration, legal_privacy, cosmetovigilance, other. Null when not yet categorised.
  secondary_category text
  request_kind text
  level smallint — 1 (simple) to 4 (serious, e.g. a skin reaction). Severity, not subject.
  responsible_team text
  language text
  happiness smallint — Customer mood read from the mail: 1 happy to 4 very unhappy.
  sender_label text — internal or contractor when one of our own addresses opened the thread; null for customer threads.
  customer_id uuid
  shopify_order_number text
  is_duplicate boolean — True when the thread is a duplicate of another ticket; exclude from volume counts.
  related_ticket_id uuid
  first_message_at timestamp with time zone
  last_message_at timestamp with time zone
  categorised_at timestamp with time zone
  investigated_at timestamp with time zone
  resolved_at timestamp with time zone
  closed_at timestamp with time zone
  case_id uuid — The customer case this thread belongs to. Several threads can share one case (a customer who wrote again on a new thread about the same problem): count support volume as count(distinct case_id), not count(*).

chat.vip_customers — One row per customer who is a VIP RIGHT NOW under the shop's VIP rule (see chat.shop: net spend strictly above vip_min_spend AND strictly more than vip_min_orders orders, both inside the last vip_window_months; uncancelled orders; marketplace orders do not count). Empty when no rule is set. Join chat.customers on customer_id for country, lifetime spend or RFM group, and chat.orders / chat.tickets on customer_id. Current status only: who was a VIP in the past cannot be reconstructed.
  customer_id uuid
  orders_in_window bigint — Uncancelled, non-marketplace orders inside the rule's window.
  net_spend_in_window numeric — Sum of total_price minus refunds on those orders (EUR).
